1. Studypool (Sell Docs) — Sab Se Behtar Option
Studypool dunya bhar ke students ke liye ek bohot bari educational website hai. Yahan aap apni AI book ko as a "Study Guide" ya "Masterclass Document" upload kar sakte hain.
• Paise Kaise Milte Hain: Har dafa jab koi user (student) aap ki PDF ko open kar ke dekhta (view karta) hai, to aap ko up to $10 (takreeban 2,700+ PKR) milte hain.
• Fayda: Aap ko khud se book bechni nahi parti, unke paas pehle hi lakhon users hain jo parhne aate hain.
2. Docsity Store
Docsity bhi ek bohot mashhoor global platform hai jahan par log informative notes, books aur guides upload karte hain.
• Paise Kaise Milte Hain: Aap apni book ka ek download rate set kar sakte hain (e.g., $2 ya $5). Jab bhi koi dunya bhar se usay download karega, aap ko paise milenge.
• Fayda: Millions of users is platform ko daily search engine ke zariye dhoondte hain.
3. File-Upload.org / Upload-4ever (PPD Cloud Storage)
Agar aap chahte hain ke aap book ka link khud social media (Facebook, GitHub, WhatsApp Groups) par share karein aur har download par paise milein, to yeh file hosting websites hain.
• Paise Kaise Milte Hain: Yeh websites har 1,000 downloads par $3 se $7 tak deti hain (isay Pay-Per-Download kehte hain).
• Fayda: User jab download karne lagta hai to usay thode ads dikhte hain, aur un ads ki kamayi ka hissa website aap ko deti hai.
💸 Pakistan Mein Payout (Paise Nikalwane Ka Tariqa)
In platforms par kaam karne ka sab se bada faida yeh hai ke yeh Pakistani users ke liye bohot flexible hain:
• Studypool & Docsity: In ki kamayi aap direct PayPal ya Payoneer ke zariye mangwa sakte hain. (Payoneer account Pakistan mein 2 minute mein ban jata hai aur direct JazzCash ke sath connect ho jata hai).
• File-Upload: Yeh platform direct JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bitcoin, aur Payoneer par bhi paise bhej deta hai.
